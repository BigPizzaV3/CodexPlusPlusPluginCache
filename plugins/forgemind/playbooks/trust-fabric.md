# Trust Fabric

Use `forgemind forge <capability>` for `trust`, `strategy`, `genome`, `flight`, `tournament`, `shrink`, `loop`, `escrow`, and `federate`. These executable capabilities provide trust attestations, strategy checks, engineering-genome recommendations, flight records, future tournaments, reversible shrink plans, product loops, evidence escrow, and k-suppressed federation. They are not separate user-facing skills; preserve their explicit hold, reject, and insufficient-evidence states.
