# Windowisp changelog

## 0.3.5 - 2026-09-04

- Added one chooser for both created v2 pets and locally installed Codex default
  pets, with Caiuto Cub retained as an independent fallback.
- Added Cinder Moth, Moss Hopper, Shellback Roller, Storm Jelly, and Gloam Golem
  to Survival alongside Ember Imp, with wave-based variety and slower movement.
- Added grounded platform movement and animation for land enemies and the boss.
- Improved rope performance and overlapping-block processing under load.
- Fixed Player 2 jumping and improved two-player Football controls.
- Fixed ladder movement allowing players to clip through adjacent blocks.
- Made Wispfall isolate existing Sandbox objects, use the smallest player size,
  and restore the player's creations after leaving the mode.
- Improved transitions between Sandbox, Survival, Dodgeball, Football, and
  Wispfall.
- Improved spring interactions across players, balls, blocks, gadgets, and
  hazards.

Living Backdrops are intentionally deferred to 0.4.0.
