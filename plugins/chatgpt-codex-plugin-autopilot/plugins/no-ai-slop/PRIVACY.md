# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-22

No AI Slop is a skills-only ChatGPT and Codex Plugin. It does not operate a publisher-hosted backend, user account service, telemetry collector, analytics endpoint, advertising service, or remote data store.

## Data collected by the publisher

None through the Plugin itself. The publisher does not receive, store, sell, or retain prompts, drafts, repository contents, credentials, or generated outputs through a No AI Slop service because no such service is operated.

## Data used during a workflow

Text, files, or other material supplied by the user may be processed by ChatGPT, Codex, or tools the user or workspace has enabled. No AI Slop provides instructions and bundled resources for those workflows but does not create an additional network destination for that data.

## External services

ChatGPT, Codex, GitHub, or other user-selected tools are separate services with their own privacy practices. No AI Slop does not silently connect to external services on its own.

## Retention

Publisher retention through No AI Slop: none. Any files, logs, or outputs stored by the host product or a user-selected connected service follow that environment's retention settings.

## User controls

Users can stop using, disable, or uninstall the Plugin at any time. Access granted to separate connected tools can be revoked through the relevant host or service.

For privacy questions, use the public support channel listed in [SUPPORT.md](./SUPPORT.md). Do not post credentials or private content in public support requests.
