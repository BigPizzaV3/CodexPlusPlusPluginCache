# Image scene patterns

Choose the smallest pattern that matches the destination. User facts and authorized brand assets override every suggestion below.

## Product and ecommerce

- **Catalog or studio:** preserve structure, use a neutral or brand-color sweep, controlled softbox reflections, contact shadow, clean margins, and no decorative clutter.
- **Lifestyle:** show credible use in a real environment; keep hand interaction, scale, and physics plausible.
- **Detail demonstration:** frame texture, mechanism, use, or surface response tightly while retaining enough context to identify the product.
- **Hero banner:** place the product on a deliberate third, create true copy space, and separate foreground from background.
- **Listing support image:** show one selling point or use case per image; avoid invented badges, dimensions, slogans, or interfaces.
- **Concept product visual:** surreal suspension, liquid, sculpture, miniature worlds, or CGI-like materials may be used only while preserving the real product and label.
- **Restyle:** lock subject structure and supplied copy; change only season, environment, palette, lighting, or styling.

## Advertising and social

- **Static ad:** one hook, one subject, one piece of visual evidence, and clear copy-safe space.
- **UGC still:** credible phone-camera height, home or street environment, natural imperfection, and realistic product handling; never invent a testimonial.
- **Carousel:** keep palette, camera, lighting, margins, and product scale consistent while giving each frame one distinct role.
- **Editorial pin:** use vertical narrative, tactile detail, layered depth, and aspirational but credible styling.
- **Campaign key visual:** create a memorable silhouette, one color mechanism, one dominant metaphor, and a simplified background.

## People and identity

- **Portrait or editorial:** define framing, gaze, expression, clothing, background, key/fill/rim relationship, and texture; preserve natural skin and facial structure unless stylization is requested.
- **Identity reference:** label each reference by role and order. Lock bone structure, skin tone, hairline, distinguishing features, and age presentation without beautifying or averaging.
- **Fashion or try-on:** preserve garment cut, material, pattern, closures, logo position, fit, anatomy, and fabric tension.

## Covers and thumbnails

- **YouTube 16:9:** one dominant face or object, a strong information gap, clear subject separation, and minimal clutter; remain truthful to the content.
- **Story or vertical cover 9:16:** keep face and action in the upper two-thirds and reserve lower interface-safe space.
- **Poster:** one visual proposition, intentional hierarchy, clean copy area, recognizable silhouette, and controlled palette.
- Extract general energy, framing, color, and layout logic from a reference thumbnail; do not copy its exact identity or composition.

## Concept selection

Offer three directions only when the user asks for brainstorming, alternatives, or concepts. When the user has requested generation, choose the most appropriate direction internally and continue; do not replace an authorized task with a proposal.

Possible directions are product fact, human outcome, and visual metaphor. For each brainstormed direction, state destination, focal subject, composition, palette, and why it communicates quickly.
