# Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 August 2026

Theisegoria's open-format file plugins are local, skills-only software packages. They do not connect to a developer-operated server, require a developer account, include developer telemetry, or send generated files to Theisegoria.

The plugins create and inspect files only in locations selected by the user or ChatGPT/Codex. They do not open, automate, or communicate with third-party desktop applications. If a user independently processes an artifact with other software, that software and ChatGPT/Codex may process data under their own settings and privacy policies.

The plugins do not intentionally collect personal information. Generated artifacts can contain user-supplied information, so users should choose appropriate storage and sharing locations.

Privacy questions may be raised through the [public support tracker](https://github.com/theisegoria/chatgpt-skill-plugins/issues).
