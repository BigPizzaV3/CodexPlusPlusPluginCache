# What You Can Do With Power BI

Choose one outcome:

1. **Build a model from data sources** — turn CSV, JSON, or any declared Power BI connector into a governed star-schema, KPI, RLS, and report-page design pack.
2. **Understand and trust KPIs** — explain definitions, data-quality caveats, and the next best analysis.
3. **Change a PBIP model safely** — assess DAX/Power Query impact and generate reviewable drafts.
4. **Release with confidence** — produce a Go/Warn/No-Go decision with tests, governance, owners, and rollback evidence.
5. **Operate Fabric** — assess capacity, freshness, Direct Lake, SLOs, and workspace risk from local or read-only snapshot evidence.
6. **Govern decisions and actions** — connect KPI evidence to owners, decision records, exception approvals, and follow-up drafts.

Start with your business outcome, not a script name. The plugin keeps PBIX/PBIT binaries untouched and labels every result by its evidence maturity.
