# Product Image Treatments: 45 Recipes

These names select creative recipes, not native PixVerse preset IDs. Read the common rules
and the selected row. Never generate the entire catalog without that request.

## Common production contract

Use the supplied or immediately relevant accepted image as the exact product
reference. If absent, obtain the image; a resolved recipe needs no new prose
brief. Default **one image, 3:4, 2K/high**. Explicit count/format wins: square 1:1,
landscape 16:9, full-screen Story/Reel 9:16. Generate and edit with Sunburst
1440p/high, retaining the accepted product as the reference.

Keep exact silhouette, proportions, construction, materials, product colors,
packaging, marks and readable labels. Preserve believable grip/anatomy, scale,
perspective, contact shadows and light/reflections. Do not invent products,
variants, claims, measurements, ingredients, certifications, prices, endorsements,
logos or watermarks. A visual metaphor is not proof of product performance.

Information cards use concise, correctly spelled type subordinate to the product.
Author exact copy once; use controlled typography/diagram composition if generated
glyphs fail. Keep the selected single-card count: supporting listing/cover/brand
skills must not turn it into their default bundle or a multi-concept campaign.
Apply normal capability/preflight/authorization and delivery rules. Keep
production prompts internal unless the user asks for prompts or an audit.

## Complete effect choices

| Treatment | Required effect and constraints |
|---|---|
| angles | ONE multi-view composition, several useful views of the same product, equal spacing and matching scale/design/color/branding. No altered or duplicated features; exact unseen views require evidence. |
| before-after | One problem→solution frame with a simple environmental contrast and the product as decisive focal solution. No fabricated medical/performance/numerical result. |
| benefits | Product-centered overview with concise labels and simple icons for visible, physically evident qualities; no unsupported numerical or regulated claims. |
| bundle | Coordinated set of actual referenced units, clear hierarchy and realistic scale. No unrelated SKUs; different products retain their own references. |
| care-guide | Clear relevant handling actions from the actual manual when care matters. Do not assume cleaning/water/heat compatibility; dry storage, gentle handling or closing apply only when appropriate. |
| color-pop | Bold layered paper, pigment or sculptural forms with depth; colors derive from the package. Keep product color and essential details intact. |
| fabric-wave | Complementary fabric curves with credible folds, tension and implied motion around the product, without obscuring essential branding. |
| feature-zoom | Full product plus ONE enlarged visible functional detail in an inset or justified cutaway. Actual construction; no invented internal technology. |
| flatlay | Precise overhead editorial grid, product anchor, a small relevant object set, clean spacing and natural shadows. |
| gym-bag | Product in/beside an OPEN gym bag with a few relevant essentials, realistic scale/folds/contact and organized lived-in styling. |
| hero-shot | Large central dominant product, simple supporting setting, strong separation, one focal hierarchy, advertising light and deliberate negative space. |
| how-it-works | Small real usage/assembly sequence with arrows or an evidenced cutaway. No invented hidden components or performance. |
| ice-capsule | Sculptural transparent ice with believable frost, bubbles, cracks and condensation; intact product remains visible and optically coherent. Concept, not durability proof. |
| in-hand | Natural actual grip, correct fingers/contact shadows/scale and source label unobstructed where possible. |
| inside-pack | Neat opened-package/evidenced cutaway showing how the product sits inside; exact exterior and mechanically credible trays/folds/compartments. |
| kitchen-scene | Credible kitchen counter use context, daylight and correct contact shadows; no unsupported ingredients or invented use. |
| luxury | Soft luminous highlights, clean deep shadows, subtle reflections and rich actual material. Glow comes from light, not a coating that hides/redesigns the product. |
| main-benefit | ONE defensible attribute expressed by one strong visual metaphor; use evidenced physical qualities when no readable claim exists. No category assumption promoted to efficacy. |
| minimalist-white | Clean neutral e-commerce field, accurate color, even light, crisp edges and natural grounding shadow. |
| moodboard | Whole central product, product-derived swatches/palette chips/context fragments/detail crops on an editorial grid. A moodboard establishes the visual direction. |
| myth-fact | TWO parts about ONE verifiable relevant misconception and its fact. No invented claim or regulated inference from appearance. |
| new-drop | Energetic arrival concept, fresh scene, package-derived colors and crisp contrast; only a legible supplied name and actual provided release facts. |
| on-desk | Contemporary usable desk, restrained relevant objects, soft directional daylight and intentionally composed lived-in arrangement. |
| pedestal-shot | Monumental conceptual product with exact geometry/branding, low horizon, architectural scale cues, atmospheric depth and restrained surroundings. |
| powder-cloud | Frozen high-speed fine-powder cloud with package-derived colors and believable density; front label readable. |
| power-angle | Dramatic low camera, realistic perspective/proportions, controlled directional light and minimal background. |
| product-match | Selection guide matching real attributes to plausible needs/contexts with neutral labels; no fabricated variants. |
| quick-steps | Exactly THREE numbered safe real-use stages, consistent product and no hidden-function assumptions. An incompatible actual procedure takes precedence over inventing a third step. |
| reel-cover | ONE STATIC cover with central product crop, mobile contrast, interface-safe space and at most a short supportable hook. No video job. |
| retail-stack | Stable stack of identical supplied packages, consistent print/scale/perspective and ONE dominant front-facing unit. |
| saveable-tip | ONE concise actionable, supportable category tip, clean hierarchy and product as relevant visual support; no invented health/financial/performance advice. |
| share-card | One shareable branded idea, concise neutral wording, repost-safe negative space and actual product-derived color/identity. |
| shelf-ready | Identifiable forward-facing product on a believable retail shelf with real depth, secondary neighbors and clean merchandising light. |
| signature-frame | Distinctive campaign composition, memorable package-derived color relationship and generous negative space; actual branding preserved. |
| specs | Precise card of supplied/visible form, finish, closure, pieces or packaging. No invented dimensions, capacity, materials or performance data. |
| swipe-opener | ONE carousel opener with product hook, flow toward next slide and short-title space, understandable alone. No unrequested full carousel. |
| symmetry | Centered, aligned spatial balance. Mirror surrounding rhythm, never asymmetric product labels/features. |
| three-reasons | Exactly THREE concise reasons from evidenced attributes, simple icons/labels and clear hierarchy. No statistics or regulated claims invented. |
| travel-pack | Credible open suitcase, carry-on tray or hotel-surface context; intact product/package, correct scale and secondary supports. |
| unboxing | ONE STATIC opened-package still, product inside/beside exact box, credible evidenced inserts/folds, no invented accessories. A video brief uses the unboxing-video skill. |
| usage-guide | Organized views of several real supported uses/presentations with consistent product; no sensitive mechanical/medical/safety assumptions. |
| vertical-hook | Vertical-first unusual crop and immediate contrast, readable product and uncluttered upper/lower interface-safe zones. Still-image output. |
| water-splash | Frozen high-speed clear-water crown, believable fluid and intact readable product. No liquid deformation/obscuring. |
| weekend-carry | Relaxed editorial arrangement with a small relevant carry set, coherent materials and strong hierarchy; no invented duplicate variants. |
| whats-inside | Breakdown of actual visible contents or legible verified package information. Unknown contents → evidenced packaging parts, never invented ingredients/accessories. |

Preserve the selected effect during repair. Reuse accepted photography for text
fixes; repair the failed interaction/view only. Planned recipes are not evidence
that all 45 have passed a render test.
