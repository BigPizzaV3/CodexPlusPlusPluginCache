# Product Motion Treatments: 18 Recipes

These names identify creative rules, not PixVerse native preset IDs. Free creative prompts keep the
ordinary direct-video policy; selecting one of these named recipes selects its
required starting-image stage. Do not omit that stage to save a task.

## Required pipeline

1. Reuse supplied/accepted exact product image. If absent, obtain it; no new
   prose brief is needed for a selected recipe. Lock shape, proportions,
   construction, packaging, colors, marks and readable labels.
2. Make ONE dedicated plate at the FINAL aspect. Center the upright whole
   product, uncropped, at **60–70% of frame**, with balanced motion-safe space.
   Use a seamless SINGLE-COLOR studio background and matching floor. Sample a
   distinctive real product/package/label/mark hue, preferring a brand accent
   over black/white/gray when present. Only lighten/darken that SAME hue for
   separation. No unrelated color or decorative gradient.
3. Soft controlled studio light, one plausible grounding shadow, exact product.
   No rotation, opening, cropping, simplification, relabeling, duplication or
   restyling. No added props/people/hands/scenery/patterns/captions/borders/logos.
   Inspect the actual plate before motion.
4. Bind the accepted plate as the actual **start frame** using a supported route,
   not just a semantic reference. Preserve its background hue except the named
   temporary light/material effect. Retain other evidence if supported.
5. Default ONE **6-second SILENT, 3:4, 1080p video**; user count/format/duration
   overrides. Use Sunburst 1440p/high for the starting plate and Seedance 2.5 1080p
   for motion. Apply only parameters exposed by the selected CLI mode.
6. Apply Seedance enhancement and prompt budgets. The CLI has no audio switch:
   request silence and **remove any audio stream at final export**, keeping the
   original clip separately. This deterministic repair needs no regeneration.

Some recipes move from a whole-product plate into closer views. A locked
start wins at frame zero: transition promptly into the named detail/move while
retaining order/duration/endpoint. If the user requires a different exact first
frame, prepare that declared opening. Never claim a complete product and extreme
close-up can both occupy the same first frame.

Preflight normally counts **one image plus one video** for a new execution.
Reuse a compatible accepted plate and follow the current PixVerse authorization
rules. The plate is a project intermediate;
deliver the requested final video under ordinary local preview/delivery rules.

## Effect-specific rules

| Treatment | Required motion and finish |
|---|---|
| crane-reveal | Low/occluded view → vertical camera rise → full balanced hero and brief stable landing. Apply the opening compatibility rule; no unexplained new prop after a locked start. |
| detail-scan | ONE continuous scan over **two or three actual visible details**, precise focus pulls, then complete product. No arbitrary cuts or hidden invented features. |
| floating-roll | Product floats with a slow graceful roll, stable axis, minimal vertical drift and soft studio reflections. Shape stays fixed; slow motion is intentional here. |
| half-turn | Camera fixed; product rotates exactly **180°**, supplied to complementary view, gentle ease-in/out and stable light. Verify actual side/back evidence. |
| impact | Stationary product → ONE controlled radial neutral-particle burst → particles freeze while camera makes a short arc. Never move/damage the product. |
| label-trace | Smooth trace across actual label/logo/type, letters stable/readable; finish focused on the full front. |
| light-sweep | Camera AND product fixed; ONE controlled light band crosses surface and reveals actual contours/materials, then settles. Reflections follow geometry. |
| liquid-wrap | One coherent fluid ribbon wraps then peels away, credible momentum/contact, no stain/bend/concealment. |
| macro-glide | Slow macro glide across actual surface/edges/print, believable scale, shallow depth and controlled focus changes. This differs from deep-focus UGC by design. |
| paint-wave | Thick sculptural paint wave passes around a CLEAN STATIONARY product. Package-derived colors; no permanent cover or relabeling. |
| product-spin | Camera fixed, product completes exactly **360°** on a clean turntable at constant angular speed, stable light and grounded shadow. Guessed unseen faces are not verified geometry. |
| pull-back | Close actual detail → smooth backward camera move → complete anchored sharp product and coherent environment. No scale morph/replacement. |
| push-in | Context → one slow steady approach → strong close hero, consistent perspective/focus/geometry and no cuts. |
| shadow-motion | Product fixed; architectural graphic shadows move slowly across field/surface under ONE coherent light source, branding readable. |
| sunrise-pass | Cool darkness → warm directional light with moving shadows → bright hero. Preserve scene/product through lighting change. |
| texture-track | Close track along ONE real texture under raking light, then widen to reconnect it to the complete product. No material replacement/exaggeration. |
| topdown-dive | Top-down → descend AND tilt into close three-quarter view in ONE continuous move, smooth horizon/spatial geometry. |
| whip-pan | Neutral adjacent area → ONE fast blurred directional pan → precise sharp product landing. Enough readable landing time; not a uniformly slow clip. |

Keep temporal edges, geometry, reflections and light coherent. No morph/melt,
replacement, extra items, people/hands, claims, captions, logos or watermarks.
End on a clean stable product frame. Inspect actual path, rotation, label and
start/end; a contact sheet cannot prove a full turn or fluid continuity. Repair
only the failed dependency; unwanted audio on intact picture needs a free export.
