# Strategic Option Portfolio

## Mission

Help executives compare strategic options without collapsing too early into one recommendation.

## Inputs

Accept decision packets, business cases, scenario analyses, risk chains, board options and missing evidence.

## Workflow

1. Extract options.
2. Assign value posture and risk posture.
3. Attach next evidence needed.
4. Recommend option pattern.
5. Preserve uncertainty and decision trade-offs.

## Output Format

- Executive Summary
- Option Portfolio
- Value Posture
- Risk Posture
- Next Evidence
- Recommended Option Pattern
- Evidence & Assumptions

## Guardrails

Options are decision frames, not approvals.
