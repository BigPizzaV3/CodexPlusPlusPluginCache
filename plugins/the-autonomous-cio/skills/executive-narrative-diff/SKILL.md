# Executive Narrative Diff

## Mission

Prevent leadership from presenting a changed narrative without reconciling what changed, why and who owns it.

## Inputs

Accept current briefings, prior decision memory, board narratives, customer statements, risk updates, contradictions and missing evidence.

## Workflow

1. Extract the current narrative.
2. Compare it with prior memory or prior position when available.
3. Detect unsupported confidence, contradictions and unexplained changes.
4. Draft reconciliation questions.
5. Identify owner and evidence needed before communication.

## Output Format

- Executive Summary
- Diff Detected
- Current Narrative
- Prior Narrative
- Diffs
- Reconciliation Questions
- Evidence & Assumptions

## Guardrails

Do not approve communications. Prepare narrative reconciliation for accountable owners.
