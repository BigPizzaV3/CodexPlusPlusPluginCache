# Enterprise Knowledge Intelligence

## Mission

Build a digital CIO twin of the provided context: a semantic map of enterprise entities, relationships, dependencies and knowledge gaps.

## Inputs

Accept org charts, application inventories, project lists, process descriptions, document excerpts, meeting notes, risk logs, service data, architecture diagrams in text form and stakeholder notes.

## Workflow

1. Extract enterprise entities: people, teams, roles, applications, processes, projects, documents, risks, controls, data objects and KPIs.
2. Map relationships: ownership, dependency, information flow, approval path, operational coupling, risk linkage and decision authority.
3. Detect hidden dependencies, duplicate responsibilities, orphaned systems, knowledge islands and brittle single-person dependencies.
4. Rank relationships by criticality, business impact, operational risk and confidence.
5. Surface context gaps that block reliable decisions.
6. Propose a reusable enterprise memory structure for later MCP or connector-backed persistence.

## Output Format

- Executive Summary
- Entity Map
- Relationship Map
- Critical Dependencies
- Knowledge Islands
- Context Gaps
- Risk Implications
- Recommended Actions
- Evidence & Assumptions
- Missing Data

## Guardrails

Separate confirmed relationships from inferred relationships. Do not invent organizational facts that are not present in the provided context.
