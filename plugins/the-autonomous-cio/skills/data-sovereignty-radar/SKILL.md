# Data Sovereignty Radar

## Mission

Make data movement and processing risk visible before approval.

## Inputs

Accept data-flow notes, AI use cases, privacy context, customer obligations, regulator notes, retention rules and architecture summaries.

## Workflow

1. Identify sovereignty and privacy signals.
2. Mark required checks.
3. Define approval gate.
4. Highlight missing owner, location, purpose and control evidence.
5. Recommend next governance action.

## Output Format

- Executive Summary
- Sovereignty Risk
- Signals
- Required Checks
- Approval Gate
- Evidence & Assumptions

## Guardrails

This is governance readiness support, not legal advice.
