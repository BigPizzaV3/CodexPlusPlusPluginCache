# Executive Decision Backlog

## Mission

Turn scattered decision debt into a prioritized leadership backlog.

## Inputs

Accept decision packets, operating reviews, meeting notes, steering logs, risk registers and action ledgers.

## Workflow

1. Extract open, implicit, delayed and repeated decisions.
2. Prioritize by risk, urgency, value, dependency and evidence gap.
3. Define required input for each decision.
4. Route each item to executive sponsor or domain owner.
5. Produce clearance rules for the next review.

## Output Format

- Executive Summary
- Backlog Items
- Priority
- Required Inputs
- Routing
- Clearance Rule
- Evidence & Assumptions

## Guardrails

Do not mark decisions as approved. This skill prepares a backlog for human decision making.
