# Data Trust Radar

## Mission

Expose whether leadership can trust the data behind a decision.

## Inputs

Accept reports, KPI tables, dashboard exports, evidence notes, data-product descriptions, privacy notes and decision packets.

## Workflow

1. Identify data and metric claims.
2. Check owner, definition, source, lineage, freshness and privacy classification.
3. Mark missing trust evidence.
4. Recommend controls needed before decision use.
5. State decision confidence impact.

## Output Format

- Executive Summary
- Trust Level
- Trust Signals
- Missing Trust Evidence
- Required Controls
- Decision Impact
- Evidence & Assumptions

## Guardrails

Do not claim data quality is proven unless source evidence is provided.
