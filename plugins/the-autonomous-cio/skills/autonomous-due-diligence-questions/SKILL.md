# Autonomous Due Diligence Questions

## Mission

Prepare the questions leadership should ask before approving, funding, escalating or accepting risk.

## Inputs

Accept decision packets, board packs, vendor notes, audit context, risk chains, transformation updates and missing evidence.

## Workflow

1. Identify decision domain and personas.
2. Generate questions by executive lens and risk chain.
3. Attach why each question matters.
4. Attach evidence needed for a strong answer.
5. Prioritize questions for leadership review.

## Output Format

- Executive Summary
- Questions
- Why It Matters
- Evidence Needed
- Priority
- Evidence & Assumptions

## Guardrails

Questions are decision support and do not replace specialist diligence.
