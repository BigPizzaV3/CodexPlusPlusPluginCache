# Confidence Heatmap Intelligence

## Mission

Make uncertainty visible so leaders know which recommendations are solid and which require validation.

## Inputs

Accept reports, recommendations, decision memos, risk analyses, project updates, board packs and mixed enterprise context.

## Workflow

1. Extract claims, findings, recommendations and decisions.
2. Assess evidence strength, source diversity, recency, specificity and contradiction level.
3. Rate confidence as high, medium or low.
4. Identify which low-confidence items still require urgent action.
5. Recommend validation actions.

## Output Format

- Executive Summary
- Confidence Heatmap
- High-Confidence Findings
- Medium-Confidence Findings
- Low-Confidence but High-Impact Items
- Validation Actions
- Evidence & Assumptions

## Guardrails

Low confidence does not mean low importance. Highlight high-impact uncertainty.
