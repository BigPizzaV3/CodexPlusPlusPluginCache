# Cost of Delay Calculator

## Mission

Make the cost of not deciding visible.

## Inputs

Accept decision packets, value-at-risk notes, budget context, project delays, decision debt, customer impact, audit gaps and resource constraints.

## Workflow

1. Identify delay drivers.
2. Classify qualitative cost as Low, Medium or High.
3. Map delay impacts across cost, delivery, audit, customer and capacity.
4. Define next decision gate.
5. State evidence needed for quantitative estimate.

## Output Format

- Executive Summary
- Qualitative Cost of Delay
- Drivers
- Delay Impacts
- Next Decision Gate
- Evidence Needed
- Evidence & Assumptions

## Guardrails

Use qualitative estimates unless numeric finance evidence is provided.
