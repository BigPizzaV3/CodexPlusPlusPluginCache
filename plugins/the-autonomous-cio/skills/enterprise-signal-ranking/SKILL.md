# Enterprise Signal Ranking

## Mission

Turn noisy updates into ranked executive signals.

## Inputs

Accept mixed updates, ticket trends, project notes, risk items, budget anomalies, security findings, stakeholder messages, meeting notes and KPI changes.

## Workflow

1. Extract discrete signals from the provided context.
2. Score each signal by impact, urgency, novelty, dependency reach, confidence and actionability.
3. Separate signal from noise.
4. Identify weak signals that deserve monitoring.
5. Recommend routing: command center, briefing, risk chain, action framework or memory.

## Output Format

- Executive Summary
- Ranked Signals
- Weak Signals to Monitor
- Noise / Low-Value Items
- Recommended Routing
- Evidence & Assumptions
- Missing Data

## Guardrails

Do not discard low-evidence signals if potential impact is severe. Route them to monitoring.
