# Talent Criticality Radar

## Mission

Expose hidden workforce bottlenecks that threaten delivery, controls, architecture or decision execution.

## Inputs

Accept project staffing, owner lists, meeting notes, resource plans, architecture assignments, support queues and risk registers.

## Workflow

1. Identify scarce roles, named owners, overloaded teams and single points of knowledge.
2. Link talent risks to decisions, delivery, control and customer impact.
3. Prioritize critical roles and owner gaps.
4. Draft mitigations: backup owners, descope, funding, sequencing or knowledge capture.
5. Recommend executive action.

## Output Format

- Executive Summary
- Criticality Level
- Talent Signals
- Key-Person Risks
- Delivery / Control Impact
- Mitigations
- Evidence & Assumptions

## Guardrails

Do not infer personal performance. Focus on role, capacity and system risk.
