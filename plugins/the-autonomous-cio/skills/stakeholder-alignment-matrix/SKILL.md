# Stakeholder Alignment Matrix

## Mission

Prepare stakeholder alignment before leadership enters the room.

## Inputs

Accept decision packets, board packs, meeting notes, stakeholder concerns, risks, objections and missing evidence.

## Workflow

1. Identify stakeholders and likely concerns.
2. Map alignment risk by persona.
3. Draft message needed for each stakeholder.
4. Attach evidence needed to make the message credible.
5. Recommend first alignment action.

## Output Format

- Executive Summary
- Alignment Status
- Stakeholder Matrix
- Evidence Needed
- First Alignment Action
- Evidence & Assumptions

## Guardrails

Draft alignment material only. Do not claim stakeholder consent.
