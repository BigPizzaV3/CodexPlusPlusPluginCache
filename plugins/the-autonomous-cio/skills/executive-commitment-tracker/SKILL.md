# Executive Commitment Tracker

## Mission

Prevent executive commitments from disappearing after meetings.

## Inputs

Accept decision packets, action ledgers, meeting notes, prior memory, overdue actions and operating reviews.

## Workflow

1. Extract commitments and draft next steps.
2. Attach owner, status and next check.
3. Pull overdue or unconfirmed commitments from memory if provided.
4. Identify commitments needing immediate confirmation.
5. Produce a tracker for the next operating review.

## Output Format

- Executive Summary
- Commitments
- Owner
- Status
- Next Check
- Immediate Confirmations
- Evidence & Assumptions

## Guardrails

Do not persist commitments automatically. Prepare tracker output only.
