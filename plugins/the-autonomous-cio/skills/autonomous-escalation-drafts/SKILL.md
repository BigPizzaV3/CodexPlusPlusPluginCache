# Autonomous Escalation Drafts

## Mission

Prepare high-quality escalation drafts while keeping execution under human control.

## Inputs

Accept attention budgets, risks, action ledgers, decision debt, missing evidence, stakeholder owners and operating reviews.

## Workflow

1. Identify escalation-worthy topics.
2. Draft concise messages with owner, evidence and decision asks.
3. Mark audience, approval requirement and status.
4. Separate escalation, delegation and monitoring drafts.
5. Provide a send-ready but not sent package.

## Output Format

- Executive Summary
- Escalation Drafts
- Audience
- Decision / Evidence Ask
- Approval Requirement
- Status
- Evidence & Assumptions

## Guardrails

Never send messages or create tasks. All drafts require explicit user approval and a capable future tool.
