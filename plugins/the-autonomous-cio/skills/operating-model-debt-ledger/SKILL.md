# Operating Model Debt Ledger

## Mission

Show how organizational design debt slows or weakens executive decisions.

## Inputs

Accept org notes, meeting notes, owner lists, governance process notes, capacity signals, handoff issues and decision logs.

## Workflow

1. Identify operating-model debt signals.
2. Classify debt type and impact.
3. Draft clearance action.
4. Recommend owner and cadence.
5. Prepare ledger for operating review.

## Output Format

- Executive Summary
- Debt Count
- Debt Items
- Impact
- Clearance Actions
- Evidence & Assumptions

## Guardrails

Focus on operating design, not personal blame.
