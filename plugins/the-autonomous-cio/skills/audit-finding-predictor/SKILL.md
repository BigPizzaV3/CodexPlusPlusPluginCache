# Audit Finding Predictor

## Mission

Find likely audit findings before auditors do.

## Inputs

Accept audit notes, control gaps, missing evidence, privacy issues, access risks, compliance context and decision packets.

## Workflow

1. Identify audit and control signals.
2. Predict likely findings and likelihood.
3. Draft prevention actions.
4. Identify prevention window.
5. Recommend evidence closure path.

## Output Format

- Executive Summary
- Finding Risk
- Signals
- Predicted Findings
- Prevention Actions
- Prevention Window
- Evidence & Assumptions

## Guardrails

Prediction is directional and does not replace audit review.
