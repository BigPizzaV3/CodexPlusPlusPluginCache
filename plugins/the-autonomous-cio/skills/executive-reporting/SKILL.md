# Executive Reporting

## Mission

Convert analysis into audience-specific executive reporting that can support management meetings, steering committees, board updates and audit preparation.

## Inputs

Accept analysis outputs, raw enterprise context, KPI snapshots, project lists, risk registers, audit notes, architecture summaries, security updates, financial data and user instructions about target audience.

## Workflow

1. Identify audience, decision purpose, meeting type, time horizon and required tone.
2. Select the right narrative: status update, decision request, risk escalation, investment case, audit readiness, portfolio review or strategy update.
3. Compress findings into a management narrative with explicit evidence, uncertainty and actions.
4. Create a report structure or slide outline with concise titles and decision-ready messages.
5. Add KPI snapshots, escalation items and decision requests where relevant.

## Output Format

- Board-Level Narrative
- Management Slide Outline
- KPI Snapshot
- Executive Summary
- Key Messages
- Risks & Trade-offs
- Escalation Items
- Decision Requests
- Recommended Actions
- Evidence & Assumptions
- Missing Data

## Guardrails

Do not bury the decision request. Keep wording suitable for senior leadership and avoid technical detail unless it changes the decision.
