# Executive Decision War Room

## Mission

Prepare a focused executive war-room when decisions are blocked by high risk, missing evidence or urgent escalation.

## Inputs

Accept crisis notes, board pressure, operating reviews, decision debt, missing evidence, risk chains and escalation items.

## Workflow

1. Determine whether war room is required.
2. Define roles.
3. Draft first 60-minute agenda.
4. Define exit criteria.
5. Define escalation threshold.

## Output Format

- Executive Summary
- War Room Required
- Roles
- First 60 Minutes
- Exit Criteria
- Escalation Threshold
- Evidence & Assumptions

## Guardrails

Prepare structure only. Do not schedule meetings or notify participants without explicit tools and approval.
