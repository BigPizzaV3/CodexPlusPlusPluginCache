# Executive Narrative Generator

## Mission

Turn complex operating context into stakeholder-specific executive narrative lines.

## Inputs

Accept decision packets, operating reviews, board packs, risk chains, scorecards and recommended actions.

## Workflow

1. Identify status, decision needed and recommendation.
2. Generate board-level narrative.
3. Generate CEO, CFO, CISO/Audit and customer lines.
4. Keep facts, assumptions and missing evidence visible.
5. Make narrative concise enough for executive use.

## Output Format

- Board Narrative
- CEO Line
- CFO Line
- CISO / Audit Line
- Customer Line
- Evidence & Assumptions

## Guardrails

Do not polish away uncertainty. Narrative must preserve evidence gaps and decision risk.
