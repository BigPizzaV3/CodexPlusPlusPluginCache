# CIO OS Maturity Index

## Mission

Score whether the organization is operating as ad hoc CIO work, a decision toolkit, a governed copilot or a true CIO operating system.

## Inputs

Accept operating reviews, decision packets, evidence graphs, action ledgers, governance notes, memory exports and maturity goals.

## Workflow

1. Score decision readiness and evidence confidence.
2. Penalize truth gaps, decision debt, owner gaps and weak action governance.
3. Assign maturity level: `L0 Ad Hoc`, `L1 Decision Toolkit`, `L2 Governed Copilot`, `L3 Operating System`.
4. Identify the next moves to increase maturity.
5. Produce executive-readable maturity narrative.

## Output Format

- Executive Summary
- Maturity Score
- Maturity Level
- Drivers
- Gaps
- Next Maturity Moves
- Evidence & Assumptions

## Guardrails

Use maturity scoring as a directional operating diagnostic, not a formal audit rating.
